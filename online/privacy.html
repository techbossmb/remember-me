Privacy at remember-me

remember-me keeps your conversations with AI assistants so they can pick up where you left off. That makes what you save here personal, and this page says plainly how it's handled.

What we store

  • Conversations you or your assistant save, word for word, grouped into projects.
  • The map of each project: topics, decisions, open questions and standing rules, each pointing back to the messages it came from.
  • Your account: the id and email address your sign-in provider gives us, and the computers you've connected.

We don't store your passwords: sign-in is handled by WorkOS, using Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Apple or an emailed code.

Where it's kept

The service runs on FastAPI Cloud and your memory is kept in a Postgres database at Neon, both in the United States (AWS, us-east-1). Traffic is encrypted, and the database is encrypted at rest by our providers.

Who can see it

  • You, and the assistants you connect to remember-me.
  • Not other users. Every request is tied to your account, and the database itself refuses to return anyone else's rows.
  • Us, only if we must. As the people running the service we can technically reach the database. Access is limited to the founder, and we look at stored content only to fix a problem you've asked us to fix, or if the law requires it.

What we don't do

  • We don't train AI models on your memory.
  • We don't sell it or share it for advertising.
  • We don't analyse its content. Conversation text is never written to our application logs or error reports.
  • Building your map is done by your own assistant, on your own plan; remember-me doesn't send your memory to any AI service.

Your controls

  • Download everything at any time from your account page, as plain files that also work offline.
  • Delete one project, or everything. Deletion removes it from the live database immediately. Our database provider keeps a short restore history for recovering from accidents; deleted data ages out of it within days.
  • Remove a connected computer, so it can no longer use your memory.
  • Keep it off our servers. With the remember-me plugin you can take projects offline onto your own computer, and choose to remove them from the cloud.

Cost

remember-me is free while it's in early access. If we introduce paid plans, we'll tell you before anything changes, and you'll always be able to download or delete your memory.
